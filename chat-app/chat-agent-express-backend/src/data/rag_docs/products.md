## Availability & Compatibility Notes

- All items are sold subject to stock. If an item is unavailable after an order is
  placed, the order (or that line item) is cancelled and refunded in full.
- Charging speeds depend on the charger and cable used; CBL-002 supports up to 60 W.
- Monitor output through HUB-003 is limited to 4K at 30 Hz and requires a host that
  supports USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode.
- Warranty periods follow the store's warranty policy: 12 months for electronics,
  6 months for accessories.
